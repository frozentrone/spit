Calculation method

Source: Saifoori, S. (2025), Analysis of Particle Deformation during Impact Deposition, PhD thesis, University of Leeds, School of Chemical and Process Engineering.

Open the thesis in White Rose eTheses

The implementation uses the empirical impact-velocity correlation reported in the source work, with C1 = 371.23, C2 = 0.464, C3 = 0.673 and C4 = 0.235.

S = ρp / ρair
νair = μair / ρair
Nd^(1/3) = D [4g(S−1)/(3νair²)]^(1/3)
Nv^(1/3) = 371.23 [Pgauge/(ρairgh)]^0.464 / [(S−1)^0.673 Nd^(0.235/3)]
Vi = Nv^(1/3) [4gνair(S−1)/3]^(1/3)

Model boundary: this is not a general CFD or force-balance solution. It is an empirical estimator for the apparatus and range from which the correlation was developed. Changing tube/nozzle geometry, gas, particle shape or operating range may invalidate the estimate.

Impact-velocity correlation source: S. Saifoori (2025), Analysis of Particle Deformation during Impact Deposition, PhD thesis, University of Leeds.

Calculation method

Source of this work
The empirical impact-velocity correlation implemented by this analyser is taken from the source below.

Source: Saifoori, S. (2025), Analysis of Particle Deformation during Impact Deposition, PhD thesis, University of Leeds, School of Chemical and Process Engineering.

Open the thesis in White Rose eTheses

The implementation uses C1 = 371.23, C2 = 0.464, C3 = 0.673 and C4 = 0.235.

S = ρp / ρair
νair = μair / ρair
Nd^(1/3) = D [4g(S−1)/(3νair²)]^(1/3)
Nv^(1/3) = 371.23 [Pgauge/(ρairgh)]^0.464 / [(S−1)^0.673 Nd^(0.235/3)]
Vi = Nv^(1/3) [4gνair(S−1)/3]^(1/3)

Breakage indices:

R* = Md/(Md+Mm) × 100%
R+ = Md/Mf × 100%
R− = (Mf−Mm)/Mf × 100%

Breakage-kernel analysis: SPIT plots the selected gravimetric breakage measure against ρdV² and fits one ordinary least-squares line through all valid repeat-level data. The fitted slope is reported as the breakability index; the x-intercept is the fitted zero-breakage transition point.

Sieve representative diameter: geometric mean = √(lower sieve × upper sieve).

# SPIT 1.2.1

Patch release: all calculated fields now refresh automatically when their dependencies change. In Breakage data this includes sieve lower/upper limits, particle density, vacuum pressure, air density, viscosity, tube length and gravity. Geometric mean and calculated impact velocity therefore update immediately. Mf/Md/Mm continue to update R*, R− and R+ live. The supporting Impact velocity tab now also recalculates automatically as its inputs change.
